Types of Attacks💻⚠️ — How Hackers Actually Get In

4 min read

 Hackers don't all use the same trick. They have different methods and knowing them helps you spot danger before it happens. Let's go through them one by one.

Click on the [+] sign next to each threat to reveal how it works

1.Malware🐛

Malware, short for malicious software, is any program intentionally designed to damage, disrupt, or gain unauthorized access to computer systems.It's a harmful program that sneaks into your device, often hidden inside an app, file, or link you download. Once inside, it can spy on you, steal your files, slow down your device, or even give the hacker full control without you knowing.

2. Phishing🎣

Phishing is when a hacker sends you a fake email, message, or link that looks real — like it's from your bank, a delivery service, or even a friend. It usually asks you to click a link or enter your password. But it's fake, and the moment you enter your details, the hacker gets them instantly.

3. Spyware👁️

Spyware is a type of program that secretly watches what you do on your device — without asking for permission. It can track your location, record what you type, or even turn on your camera and microphone without you noticing. Most of the time, it hides inside apps that look completely normal, so you'd never suspect it's there.

4. Ransomware💰

Ransomware is one of the scariest types of attacks. It locks up your files or your entire device, and then the hacker demands money to unlock it. If you don't pay, you may lose access to your photos, documents, or important data forever. Even paying doesn't always guarantee you'll get it back.

Now You Know the Threats

Phishing, malware, spyware, ransomware — these are just some of the ways hackers try to get in. They all work differently, but the goal is always the same: getting your data, money, or control without your permission.The good news? Once you know how these attacks work, they become much easier to spot and avoid. Knowledge really is your first line of defense.
