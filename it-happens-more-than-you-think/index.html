It Happens More Than You Think

3 min read

If you think password leaks only happen to big companies or careless people, think again. Massive data breaches happen almost every month, hitting websites you use daily, shopping sites, social media platforms, even email providers.

How Often Does This Really Happen?

Billions of login credentials have been exposed over the years, sitting in leaked databases that anyone can access if they know where to look. Chances are, at least one of your old passwords is already out there somewhere.

Why This Matters to You

Even if you don't reuse passwords intentionally, many people do without realizing it. And once a hacker gets one leaked password, they'll try it everywhere else you might have an account.
