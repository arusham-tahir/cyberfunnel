The Bottom Line

2 min read

You started this journey wondering if free WiFi was really free ... and now you know the real cost isn't always paid in money. It's paid in privacy, if you're not careful. You've learned how attackers hide in plain sight, how they slip into your connection, and most importantly, how a few simple habits can keep you protected.

Public WiFi isn't something to fear. It's something to use wisely. Every time you turn on a VPN, check for HTTPS, or skip logging into your bank on an open network, you're making yourself a harder target. The internet doesn't stop being useful just because it comes with risks. It just means you use it a little smarter, one habit at a time.

"Free WiFi will always be free. Just make sure you're not the one paying for it."
