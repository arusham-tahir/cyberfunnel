Privacy Policy

Who We Are

Cyber Funnel is an educational website created to help beginners understand basic cybersecurity concepts, including phone privacy, WiFi safety, and online protection habits. Our website address is: https://arusham-tahir.github.io/cyberfunnel/

Data Collection

This website is a static site and does not collect, store, or process any personal data from visitors. There are no forms, no user accounts, no login systems, and no comment sections on this site.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies to track or identify visitors.

Third-Party Links

Some pages on this site may include links to external websites or resources (such as recommended courses). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party sites. We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of any external site they visit.

Embedded Content

This site does not currently embed content from third-party platforms (such as videos or social media posts). If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website. Any updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, feel free to reach out via our GitHub profile: https://github.com/arusham-tahir
